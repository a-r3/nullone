## Recommended Texbrif style

Differentiation = **AI-first explainability**, not breadth or tutorial volume.

- Format mix target: **carousel (explainers/lists) + single-image news brief + Story (breaking/follow-up)**; Reel only when motion adds value.
- Hook: number-led statement, with a one-line "why it matters" (the differentiator references under-use).
- Caption: emoji + hook + 2–4 concise fact lines + optional "why it matters" + 3–6 hashtags at end.
- Visual: clean text-led cards + official/source images; avoid meme/screenshot-heavy and sponsored look.
- Localization: AZ-first, convert USD→manat where useful.

## Concrete style rules (v2)

1. Open every caption with 1–2 emoji.
2. Anchor every hook on a specific, verified number.
3. Use declarative statements as default; questions only for explainers/tutorials.
4. Place hashtags at the end (3–6, mixed Azerbaijani + EN/AI).
5. Keep captions to a news-brief shape: hook → 2–4 fact lines → "why it matters".
6. Add a one-line "why it matters" — the differentiator references skip.
7. Use carousels for explainers, lists, comparisons, and daily digests.
8. Structure any digest carousel: cover ("swipe left" + date) → one slide per item → closing slide.
9. Use Story for breaking/one-fact updates and follow-ups.
10. Use Reel only when motion/demo footage materially improves the post.
11. Prefer official/source visuals and screenshots over AI-generated media.
12. Keep cards text-led but low-density (one headline + one number max per slide).
13. Localize: Azerbaijani-first, convert USD→manat where useful.
14. Flag rumors/leaks explicitly; never present unverified claims as fact.
15. Distinguish can / will / plans to / testing / preview / available precisely.
16. Never copy reference captions, cards, logos, or wording.
17. Do not manufacture weak posts to hit a 2/day quota.
18. Keep visual identity consistent (one template family), unlike references' mixed look.
19. Avoid sponsored/tutorial-Reel content until the audience is established.
20. Track performance per post (topic, hook, format, time) and revisit this model with real data.
