# NullOne Content Rules

## Current autonomy

Mode: DRAFT_FIRST

The agent may autonomously:
- monitor sources
- monitor reference accounts
- identify topics
- research facts
- rank topics
- choose formats
- find usable source visuals
- prepare feed posts
- prepare carousels
- prepare Stories
- prepare publication-ready drafts

Do not autonomously publish yet.

## Quality over quota

Target 2 feed publications per day.

A third publication is allowed when the topic is genuinely strong.

If only one strong topic exists, publish-quality preparation is more important
than filling the remaining quota.

## Content selection

Prefer developments that are:
- recent
- useful
- surprising or consequential
- relevant to Azerbaijani technology/AI audiences
- explainable clearly
- supported by credible sources

Avoid:
- empty hype
- meaningless minor product changes
- obvious engagement bait
- unverified rumors presented as facts
- rewriting another Azerbaijani media page

## AI focus

Artificial intelligence is the primary editorial vertical.

Prioritize:
- major model/product releases
- useful new AI tools
- AI agents
- important OpenAI / Google / Anthropic / Meta / Microsoft developments
- practical AI use cases
- major research breakthroughs
- AI safety/security when relevant
- AI business and investment developments
- important changes affecting ordinary users
- notable open-source AI developments

## Technology

Also cover selectively:
- major devices
- operating systems
- software/platform changes
- cybersecurity
- chips and computing
- startups
- technology business
- notable Azerbaijan technology developments

## Format logic

Use Story for:
- breaking updates
- one-fact updates
- polls/questions
- quick follow-ups
- teaser/context
- items useful but not strong enough for the feed

Use carousel for:
- explainers
- lists
- comparisons
- multi-step developments
- topics needing context

Use a normal feed post for:
- a strong single news item
- concise important development
- visually obvious product/update

Use Reel only when video materially improves communication
or suitable source/demo footage exists.

## Media

Prefer:
1. official press/product assets
2. official screenshots
3. screen captures/recordings
4. source imagery usable for editorial presentation
5. simple reusable NullOne templates
6. generated images/video only when genuinely necessary

Generation is NOT the default.

## Originality

Never reproduce reference-account captions or creative layouts verbatim.

Research the underlying story independently and produce original Azerbaijani copy.

## Factual standard

For consequential claims:
- seek the original/primary source where possible
- corroborate if uncertainty exists
- identify rumor/leak status clearly
- do not fabricate numbers, quotations or capabilities

## Mandatory pre-publication verification

Every feed post, carousel and factual Story must pass a final verification step
AFTER drafting and BEFORE publication.

The verifier must compare the final Azerbaijani wording against the cited source,
not merely verify that the general story exists.

Specifically check:

- Do not broaden a claim beyond what the source says.
- Do not merge separate products, integrations or features into one.
- Do not infer relationships such as "successor", "replacement", "partnership",
  "acquisition" or "launch" unless supported by a source.
- Qualify statements that apply only to a certain plan, country, user group,
  rollout stage or product tier.
- Every important number must match the source.
- Distinguish "can", "will", "plans to", "testing", "preview", and "available".
- If the primary source and secondary reporting differ, prefer the primary source
  and note the uncertainty.

Before marking content publication-ready, assign:

VERIFICATION: PASS
or
VERIFICATION: BLOCKED

Automatic publishing may only publish items with VERIFICATION: PASS.

## Instagram text formatting

Instagram captions are plain text.

Before creating a Zernio draft or publishing:
- remove Markdown syntax such as **bold**, *italic*, headings and code fences
- preserve normal Unicode, emojis, line breaks and hashtags
- optimize the first ~125 characters because they appear before the caption fold
- never let formatting artifacts such as ** or ## reach Instagram

## Human approval gate

Current mode remains DRAFT_FIRST.

A Zernio draft may be published only when the operator explicitly approves
that exact Zernio post ID.

Never interpret:
- general approval of the system
- "looks good"
- successful validation
- successful draft creation
as authorization to publish.

Explicit publication authorization must identify the intended draft/post.

Before publishing:
1. GET the Zernio post again.
2. Confirm status is draft.
3. Confirm account is @nullone.az.
4. Confirm factual VERIFICATION is still PASS.
5. Publish only that one post.
6. Read back the resulting platform status and Instagram URL.

Never create another duplicate post when an existing approved draft can be promoted.

## Mandatory Instagram canvas sizes

All NullOne media must be prepared in exact publication dimensions.

### Feed post
- Exact canvas: 1080 × 1350 px
- Aspect ratio: 4:5
- This is the default and preferred NullOne feed format.

### Carousel
- Exact canvas for EVERY slide: 1080 × 1350 px
- Aspect ratio: 4:5
- Every slide in one carousel must use the same dimensions.
- Do not mix portrait, square, and landscape slides.

### Story
- Exact canvas: 1080 × 1920 px
- Aspect ratio: 9:16

### Reel
- Exact video canvas: 1080 × 1920 px
- Aspect ratio: 9:16

## Source-media handling

Never publish a source image merely because Instagram/Zernio technically accepts
its original dimensions.

If an official/source image has another aspect ratio:
1. preserve the important source image without destructive cropping;
2. place it inside the correct NullOne canvas;
3. use surrounding space for headline, context, branding or neutral background;
4. maintain visual hierarchy and legibility;
5. export the final asset at the exact required dimensions.

Example:
A 1600×900 official image intended for a feed post must be composed into
a 1080×1350 NullOne asset before publication.

Do not stretch images.
Do not distort logos.
Do not crop away important UI, text, people, products or factual context.

## Validation

Before Zernio preflight or draft creation, verify:
- pixel width
- pixel height
- aspect ratio
- file format
- file size

A feed asset that is not exactly 1080×1350 is BLOCKED.
A Story/Reel asset that is not exactly 1080×1920 is BLOCKED.
A carousel containing slides with inconsistent dimensions is BLOCKED.

## Source asset selection quality

A technically valid official image is not automatically a good Instagram asset.

Prefer, in order:
1. official product/person/device/event visual
2. useful official screenshot
3. clean official visual with little or no embedded text
4. company/logo composition built editorially
5. official text-card only when nothing better exists

Avoid using an English-language source text-card as the main visual when it
merely repeats the Azerbaijani headline.

The final NullOne composition must look Instagram-native, not like a document,
website screenshot, or internal report.

## Carousel readability

A carousel is not an article broken into screenshots.

Before rendering:
- remove unnecessary explanatory sentences
- prefer one claim per slide
- body copy should normally fit in 1-3 short lines
- major numbers should be visual elements, not buried inside sentences
- if a slide needs small body text to fit, rewrite or split it

At thumbnail and normal phone viewing size, the primary message must remain obvious.
