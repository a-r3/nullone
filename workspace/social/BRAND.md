## Visual identity

Default NullOne composition:
- near-black background
- warm cream typography
- Signal Orange as the only recurring primary accent
- strong editorial hierarchy
- generous negative space
- authentic source imagery may retain its original colors

Signal Orange should usually occupy only a small part of the composition.

Use it for:
- key numbers
- important words
- labels
- data/chart highlights
- dividers
- visual signals
- selected UI-style details

Do not turn every slide orange.

Avoid as recurring brand language:
- cyan
- teal
- generic electric blue
- neon AI glow
- excessive gradients
- crypto/fintech aesthetics
