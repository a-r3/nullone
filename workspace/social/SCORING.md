# NullOne Editorial Scoring v1.1

Score candidates before committing production resources.

The scoring system is CONTENT-TYPE NEUTRAL.

A candidate must not be penalized merely because it is an EXPLAINER,
PRACTICAL or EVERGREEN rather than fresh NEWS.

Each positive dimension: 0–5.

## Positive dimensions

RELEVANCE ×2
How closely does this fit NullOne's AI/technology audience?

FRESHNESS ×2
How timely is this content for ITS OWN CONTENT TYPE?

For NEWS/BREAKING:
event recency and urgency matter.

For EXPLAINER/PRACTICAL:
score whether the subject is currently relevant/useful.

For EVERGREEN:
score present-day usefulness, not the age of the underlying concept.

A durable topic should not receive FRESHNESS=0 merely because it is durable.

USEFULNESS ×2
Does the reader learn, understand or gain something practical?

CONSEQUENCE ×2
Could this materially affect users, industry, developers, business or society?

SOURCE QUALITY ×2
How strong/direct is the evidence?

NOVELTY ×1
Does the angle add something genuinely new, surprising or clarifying?

AZERBAIJAN FIT ×1
Does this have meaningful relevance to an Azerbaijani audience?

VISUAL FIT ×1
Can it become a strong Instagram-native piece?

Maximum positive score: 65.

---

## Penalties

SATURATION ×2
0 = angle remains useful/distinct
5 = audience has probably seen this exact angle repeatedly

UNCERTAINTY ×3
0 = confirmed
5 = highly speculative

EVIDENCE WEAKNESS ×3
0 = strong primary evidence
5 = poor sourcing

FINAL SCORE =
positive score
- saturation penalty
- uncertainty penalty
- evidence weakness penalty

---

## Initial production thresholds

FEED:
Final score >= 38

CAROUSEL:
Final score >= 42
and multiple slides must materially improve understanding.

STORY:
Final score >= 26

BREAKING:
May move rapidly, but factual verification is never skipped.

Below threshold:
Usually reject or retain/defer.

---

## Editorial selection layer

FINAL SCORE is not the only selection input.

When several READY candidates pass threshold, Draft Factory must also consider:

- today's already prepared/published main count
- current rolling content-type balance
- topic-cluster saturation
- audience usefulness
- distinctiveness
- source confidence
- format suitability
- freshness appropriate to content type

Do NOT add arbitrary bonus points merely to force content-mix percentages.

A strong EXPLAINER may legitimately outrank mediocre NEWS.

Quality overrides quota.

---

## Hard blocks

Regardless of score, BLOCK if:

- central claim cannot be verified
- headline materially exaggerates source
- rumor is presented as fact
- required limitation changes the meaning of the story
- visual contains misleading/fabricated factual information
- comparison uses incompatible metrics without disclosure
- practical instructions claim unavailable functionality
- exact media dimensions fail
- required publication validation fails

Final publication-ready state requires:

VERIFICATION: PASS
